Outdoor Day Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Outdoor Day (“the App”) is published by Lets Hang LLC.

Location

The App requests your device location while in use to fetch local UV index, pollen, and air quality. Location is used on-device to call weather/air-quality APIs and is not stored on our servers.

Ads

If you have not purchased Outdoor Day Plus, the App may show ads via Google AdMob. Ad providers may collect device identifiers according to their policies. Purchasing Outdoor Day Plus removes ads.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details.
