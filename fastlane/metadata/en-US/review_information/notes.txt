Outdoor Day is a utility that shows a simple outdoor verdict from location-based UV, pollen, and US AQI (Open-Meteo).

Demo:
1. Grant location when prompted (or deny and use the last known / error state).
2. Home screen shows Go / Go with care / Better stay in plus UV, pollen, and AQI chips.
3. Banner ads appear at the bottom for free users (Google test units in this build).
4. Tap Outdoor Day Plus to purchase the non-consumable remove-ads unlock (StoreKit product us.letshang.outdoor_day.remove_ads). Sandbox account can be used.

No account required. Not medical advice.
