Outdoor Day is a utility that shows a simple outdoor verdict from location-based UV, pollen, and US AQI (Open-Meteo + US pollen fallback).

Demo:
1. Grant location when prompted (or deny — the app shows a location-off message).
2. Home screen shows Go / Go with care / Better stay in plus UV, pollen, and AQI chips.
3. App Tracking Transparency may appear once (AdMob). Choose either option; ads still load with Google test units in this build.
4. Free users see a bottom banner and an “Unlock Outdoor Day Plus” card.
5. Tap Unlock Outdoor Day Plus to buy the non-consumable us.letshang.outdoor_day.remove_ads ($2.99) — removes ads and unlocks the 3-day outlook. Use a Sandbox Apple ID.

No account required. Not medical advice. Export compliance: ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption=false.
